# FC-01 v1.3 Amendment B limitations

- The authenticated interactive task session is the accepted Davide identity
  boundary. The packet validator can prove exact bytes, syntax, scope, time,
  and state; it cannot independently prove the human identity behind a user
  message.
- This package activates remediation only. It does not authorize a production
  transaction, live M00, Voice, database mutation, secret mutation, main merge,
  PR #144 mutation, or canonical promotion.
- Amendment A remains historically sealed and byte-identical. Its approval
  evaluator is expected to keep reporting its historical no-decision state and
  is no longer the effective campaign readiness command.
- Current product, provider, database, privacy, secret, and test blockers are
  not declared resolved by this governance amendment. Each still requires the
  direct evidence and disposition inherited from Amendment A.
- The old Amendment A repair and candidate budgets never activated and cannot
  be reused. D1 must bind a fresh target transaction budget; D2 and D3 bind
  their own distinct scopes.
- The fixed expiry is fourteen wall-clock days from the B seal and never
  pauses. The separate inherited twenty-four-active-engineering-hour counter
  pauses only during immutable WAITING_FC01_D1, WAITING_FC01_D2, and
  WAITING_FC01_D3 intervals. A committed start receipt precedes repair-branch
  creation; packet presentation and consumption each require independent
  recomputation. Expiry of either clock is BLOCKED_BUDGET.
- Font source and license URLs identify authoritative upstreams, but the actual
  committed asset bytes, release/tag identities, licenses, and SHA-256 values
  must be proved on the repair candidate.
- Any unknown, not-run, drifted, or confidential prerequisite remains blocking.
  No gate is waived by Davide-only governance.
- “Safely blocked” is never a D1 PASS value. Secret-history, tracked-record,
  ACL/RLS/data, route, identity, test, and font/experience prerequisites must
  have their exact sealed PASS receipts or the campaign stops.
