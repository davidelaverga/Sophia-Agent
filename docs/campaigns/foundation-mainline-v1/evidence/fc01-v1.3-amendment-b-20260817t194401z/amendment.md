its SHA-256, presents it in the active task, and stops. Only a later

each gate;
packet identifiers and nonces are globally unique. An expired, rejected, or
withdrawn packet does not authorize a replacement hidden chain. The evaluator

If the active-engineering limit, absolute mission expiry, or a packet/approval
window expires before another typed terminal outcome exists, the evaluator
