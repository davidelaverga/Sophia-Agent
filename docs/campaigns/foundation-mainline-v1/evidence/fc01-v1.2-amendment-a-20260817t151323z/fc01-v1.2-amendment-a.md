## 2. Deadlock resolution without waiver

FC-01A-R resolves the governance deadlock by separating causal reachability to
the frozen M00 text/Builder/native-PPTX envelope from reachability to later
FC-01 release scenarios.

- `M00_PREREQUISITE` means the blocker can affect M00 product truth, its exact
  candidate or evidence, or a mandatory security/privacy precondition. Each
  must become `PASS` no later than its exact `required_before_state` phase in
  `blocker-reachability.json`, and all must be `PASS` before
  `BASELINE_FROZEN` or any M00 execution. A proof that can exist only after a
  candidate deployment is not circularly required before that deployment.
- `POST_M00_RELEASE_GATE` means exact evidence proves the blocker is outside
  the frozen M00 execution path. It remains failed and must be resolved before
  its original G2, G3, G4, G5, M01, or release boundary.
- Classification never changes severity, calls a failure passed, accepts a
  defect, or waives P0, P1, or P2. No waiver field exists.
- Unknown or conditional reachability defaults to `M00_PREREQUISITE` until a
  checksum-bound receipt proves otherwise.
- Any confirmed secret, personal data, cross-user access, destructive change,
  or unavailable rollback for a currently participating/applicable target is
  P0 and stops the lane regardless of class. A rollback for a surface proven
  unreachable from M00 remains blocking at its exact later boundary and
  escalates immediately if that surface becomes applicable.

The complete and disjoint classification is normative in
`blocker-reachability.json`. The exact two P1 and eight P2 review IDs remain
the complete source-authorized review set. All ten must be repaired and reach
`PASS` in FC-01A-R, including the two that are not causally exercised by M00; this
prevents their temporal classification from becoming acceptance or debt.

## 10. Nullable provider metadata

Future BASE-00 receipts must not encode an unavailable value as the string
`UNKNOWN`. The FC-01 service schema permits `build_or_image_digest`,
`boot_or_connection_epoch`, and an inapplicable `durable_store_kind` to be JSON
`null` only with `availability_status`, nonempty reason, evidence basis, and
observation time.

Only `PROVIDER_NOT_EXPOSED` and `NOT_APPLICABLE` may accompany null.
`NOT_COLLECTED` or `ACCESS_FAILED` remains a hard blocker. Vercel retention
duration is supplemental operational metadata rather than service identity and
may be null after authenticated non-exposure is recorded.

The five predecessor image/build/retention unknowns are therefore
`RESOLVED_NULLABLE` and are removed from the blocker register; they do not
become later gates. Vercel's build/image field is
`PROVIDER_NOT_EXPOSED`—not `NOT_APPLICABLE`—because Vercel does perform a
build. Render boot epochs may likewise be null only after authenticated
non-exposure is recorded.

Participating service/source identity, deployment ID, route, status,
observation time, and exact rollback source/deployment/selectability remain
non-null. Voice rollback is not nullable. LangGraph's live instantiated
checkpointer class remains a substantive post-M00/M01 blocker even though the
normalized `durable_store_kind` field may be null with a reason.

## 11. Evidence and abort law

Every repair follows:

```text
exact failure and immutable candidate
→ one invariant
→ one causal hypothesis
→ smallest allowlisted change
→ failed gate
→ regression neighborhood
→ exact-head candidate gate
→ checksum-bound receipt
```

Abort `BLOCKED` when any of the following occurs:

- frozen source, predecessor control head, authority, or evidence digest drifts;
- a change escapes the finding/path/test allowlist;
- a repair requires Voice participation, M01 behavior, migration, DDL/DML,
  secret rotation, provider setting, paid infrastructure, or broader feature
  work;
- a confidential review finds a real secret or personal record;
- any M00 relation/RPC/project/ACL/RLS/storage requirement fails or is unproved;
- any M00 prerequisite remains `FAIL`, `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_RUN`, or skipped;
- the same invariant fails after two scoped cycles;
- exact candidate build or CI cannot execute;
- the M00 prompt, identity, one-repair ceiling, provider/model policy, or rubric
  changes;
- a deployment becomes necessary without a target-specific approval;
- a restart/disconnect occurs while LangGraph durability is unproved; or
- a new P0, P1, or M00-scope P2 appears.
