# Deck Hallmark

Use these rules for presentation planning and pre-prepare critique:

1. Score Philosophy, Hierarchy, Execution, Specificity, Restraint, and Variety
   from 1 to 5. Revise the weakest point, then bring every final score to at
   least 3.
2. Derive palette, typography, materials, diagrams, vocabulary, and texture
   from the subject. "Professional" and "technical" are quality constraints,
   not visual styles.
3. Reject repeated card grids, repeated eyebrow-plus-number scaffolds, and a
   single template fingerprint across the deck.
4. Keep one coherent token system while varying composition and spatial rhythm.
5. Never invent evidence, metrics, quotations, or factual relationships to fill
   a visual slot.
6. Remove decoration that has no semantic or subject-specific purpose.

Do not apply Hallmark's web-specific navigation, hero, footer, responsive,
mobile, motion, component-state, SVG, or browser workflow rules to slides.
Fresh deck execution remains service-owned through `prepare_deck_build`.
