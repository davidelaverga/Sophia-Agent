# Sophia Anti-Slop Rubric

Use this check before creating or emitting any visual artifact. A technically valid output is not finished if it looks generic, repetitive, or careless.

## Six Axes

- **Philosophy**: The artifact must feel deliberately designed for this specific topic, audience, and medium; never like a generic template filled with nouns.
- **Hierarchy**: A viewer should know the primary idea in two seconds. Every page or slide needs one clear focal point, then supporting structure.
- **Execution**: Alignment, spacing, contrast, cropping, and text rendering must be clean. No overlap, clipped labels, blurry assets, garbled text, or cramped layouts.
- **Specificity**: Use the real subject, real claims, real labels, and real data. Placeholder language, "Item 1", vague icons, and fake values fail.
- **Restraint**: Remove anything that does not earn its place. Avoid clutter, redundant decorations, crowded legends, and repeated visual tricks.
- **Variety**: Do not repeat the same treatment section after section or slide after slide. Vary structure while keeping one coherent visual language.

## Universal Anti-Patterns

- No purple/pink AI-gradient hero.
- No single-font, single-layout template look.
- No generic stock-deck styling.
- Glossy 3D, clip-art, emoji, literal AI cliches, busy tech textures, or decorative borders.
- Data visuals without explicit labeled data.
- Decorative diagrams where a paragraph, table, or stat callout would be clearer.

Fail the artifact and repair it if any of these are present.
